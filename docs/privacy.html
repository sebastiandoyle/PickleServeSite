Privacy Policy

Effective: Today

PickleServe is a one-device, offline pickleball scorekeeper. We do not collect, track, or transmit any personal data.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No accounts, logins, or user identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No location, contacts, photos, or health data
  • No network calls or cloud storage

On‑Device Only

All scoring and settings are handled locally on your device. The app works entirely offline.

Third‑Party SDKs

None.

Children’s Privacy

PickleServe is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information.
